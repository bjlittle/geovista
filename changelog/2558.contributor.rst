Renamed :bash:`tests/themes/test.py` to :bash:`tests/themes/test_themes.py`.
The old name matched neither half of the ``pytest`` ``python_files`` default,
``test_*.py`` and ``*_test.py``, so none of its eleven tests had been collected
since they landed.

Collecting them exposed a real failure. ``test_default_theme`` asserts that the
global ``pyvista`` theme is ``geovista``, and importing :bash:`tests/plotting`
loads the ``pyvista`` testing theme over it to prepare off-screen rendering.
That happens at import, so collecting the image tests displaces the theme
whether or not they are then selected to run. The test now reinstates the theme
it is about through a fixture, and puts back whatever it found afterwards.
