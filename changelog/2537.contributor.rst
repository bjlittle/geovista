Declared ``make`` as a dependency of the ``docs``
`pixi <https://github.com/prefix-dev/pixi>`__ 🧚 feature. Every task in that
feature shells out to it, as do the ``tests-doc`` and ``tests-docs-browser``
tasks by way of ``depends-on``, so a contributor whose host lacked ``make``
could not build, serve or test the documentation at all - each task exited
``127`` from its ``clean`` step. The :fab:`github` runner images ship ``make``,
which is why CI never saw it. See :issue:`2535`. (:user:`claude`)
