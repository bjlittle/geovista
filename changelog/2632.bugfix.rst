Fixed :meth:`geovista.bridge.Transform.from_1d` ignoring a mask on its bounds.
A masked bound was used as its fill value, or became a NaN point in the mesh,
with no warning. It is a gap in a contiguous axis, so ``from_1d`` now raises a
``ValueError`` that names the axis and counts the masked bounds. Closes
:issue:`2627`. (:user:`claude`)
