Fixed :func:`geovista.common.from_cartesian` reading a surface at the mean
radius of its points, which gave the wrong latitudes for one whose points sit
at uneven radii, such as a surface :func:`geovista.transform.transform_mesh`
lifts by a ``zlevel`` at each point onto the sphere. Each point of such a
surface is now read at its own radius. Closes :issue:`2670`. (:user:`claude`)
