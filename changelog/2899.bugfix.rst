Fixed :meth:`geovista.bridge.Transform.from_points` drawing a point at the value
under a mask, or a NaN point, for a position whose x or y value is masked or NaN,
and raising a ``TypeError`` for a ``None``. It now drops each such point before it
transforms the rest, along with its entries in ``data``, ``vectors``, the heights
and a ``zlevel`` array, and warns once, counting the points dropped. A cloud with
no points, whether every point was missing or none was given, now keeps its
``vectors``, where it raised a ``ValueError``, and keeps its arrays when cleaned.
:meth:`geovista.geoplotter.GeoPlotterBase.add_points` cuts its ``scalars`` by the
same points. :class:`geovista.geodesic.BBox` now builds from corners given as a
grid, taking them in order, where its mesh failed on an assertion. This lands
row 3 of positions spec §9.
