Added the ``floors-docs`` environment and a weekly ``docs`` job to ``ci-floors.yml``,
landing row 5 of floors spec §9. The job builds the documentation with each ``docs``
dependency at its floor, on the oldest supported Python, and fails on a warning. A
failing scheduled run opens an issue, or comments on the open one. The build disproved
three floors, now raised on both sides: ``sphinx`` to 9.0.4, ``sphinx-autoapi`` to
3.8.1 and ``sphinxcontrib-bibtex`` to 2.6.4. Raising ``sphinx`` past 9 also raises
``sphinx-design`` to 0.7.0, the lowest release that accepts it. The
``geovista.themes`` docstring now links ``set_plot_theme`` where the API reference
documents it. (:user:`claude`)
