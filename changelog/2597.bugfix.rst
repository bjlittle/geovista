``slice_lines`` now carries a line mesh's point and cell data across the
antimeridian, so a line crossing the seam keeps its data in a projected plot.
Each point it adds takes the values interpolated at the crossing, a duplicated
vertex keeps its own, and both halves of a split segment keep its cell data.
Every array keeps its role, such as the active scalars or pedigree ids, and any
point or cell added gets a global id of its own. ``transform_mesh`` can now
carry a per-point ``zlevel`` along such lines too. Closes :issue:`2583`.
