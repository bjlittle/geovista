Added the ``test-floors`` job to ``ci-wheels.yml``, landing row 4 of floors spec §9.
On each ``v*`` tag, and by hand, it installs the built wheel with its ``test`` and
``exam`` extras at their ``pip`` floors and runs the tests without the image tests.
``publish-artifacts-pypi`` now waits for it, so no release reaches PyPI until its
wheel passes at its floors. ``uv`` takes a wheel's own requirements as transitive, so
the ``floors-pypi`` action now passes them to it as direct requirements. The action
also sets ``PYVISTA_OFF_SCREEN`` and ``GEOVISTA_POOCH_MUTE`` itself, since
``ci-wheels.yml`` sets neither. No floor was raised. (:user:`claude`)
