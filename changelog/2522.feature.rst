Added :func:`geovista.crs.planar`, which determines whether a
:class:`~pyproj.crs.CRS` renders a scene as a flat plane. A scene is rendered on
the surface of a 3D sphere only for :data:`geovista.crs.WGS84`, and flat
otherwise - the same rule applied by :func:`geovista.transform.transform_mesh`.
:func:`geovista.crs.projected` now asks this same question of a mesh, deferring
to :func:`~geovista.crs.planar` whenever the mesh carries a
:class:`~pyproj.crs.CRS`. (:user:`claude`)
