Published the
:doc:`open items design specification </developer/specs/2026-11-02-open-items-design>`.
Each gap found while delivering a specification becomes one of its open items,
with an issue of its own labelled ``spec: open item``, and names the roadmap row
that owes it. A row can't land past an item it owes unless its pull request
settles, moves or promotes the item, and an item that has moved twice graduates
to a roadmap row of its own. (:user:`claude`)
