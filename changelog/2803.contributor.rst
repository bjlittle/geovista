Published the open items design specification. Each gap found while delivering a
specification becomes one of its open items, with an issue of its own labelled
``spec: open item``, and names the roadmap row that owes it. A row can't land
past an item it owes unless its pull request settles, moves or promotes the
item, and an item that has moved twice graduates to a roadmap row of its own.
