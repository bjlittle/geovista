Made each decision of a specification citeable, as ``heights spec §2 decision 3``,
with an anchor the build gives it. The docs, citation and decision ledger
specifications now record each decision with who agreed it and when, and end with
a Verification table naming the test or the sign-off behind each one. The
conventions test holds every specification in the ledger to it, and the
specifications index explains how to add, retire, verify and sign off a decision.
