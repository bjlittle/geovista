``tests/test_dependency_ceilings.py`` checks that every pixi ceiling in
``pyproject.toml`` admits at least what its counterpart in
``requirements/pypi-*.txt`` admits. ``dependabot`` raises only the pip
ceilings, so a pixi ceiling left behind kept the lock on the old release, and
CI never tested what a ``pip`` user could install. The check fails on the
``dependabot`` pull request itself, which is where the pixi bump now goes.
