and ``pandas-stubs`` from pandas, to the ``devs`` environment, so ``mypy`` can check
``geovista``'s use of those libraries against their real interfaces. Its use of

``geovista.pantry.data`` leaves the typing ratchet. ``geovista.cache``,
``geovista.cli``, ``geovista.config``, ``geovista.geometry`` and ``geovista.qt``
leave it in this change. (:user:`claude`)
