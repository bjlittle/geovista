Fixed :meth:`geovista.bridge.Transform.from_unstructured` raising ``ProjError``
for a masked point outside the domain of a bounded projection, such as an
off-disk fill value in ``+proj=ortho``. The points that it drops from their
faces, when ``xs`` and ``ys`` carry the same mask, are no longer transformed.
They stay in the mesh at the position of the first point that isn't masked, so
their fill values no longer stretch its bounds. Closes :issue:`2603`.
(:user:`bjlittle`)
