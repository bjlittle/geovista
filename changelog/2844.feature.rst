The public functions that take longitudes and latitudes, coordinates in a CRS,
or the angles of a graticule take them as ``pint`` quantities from any registry
too, as the builders of :class:`geovista.bridge.Transform` do. Among them are
:func:`geovista.transform.transform_points`, :class:`geovista.geodesic.BBox`,
:func:`geovista.geodesic.line`, :meth:`geovista.search.KDTree.query`,
:meth:`geovista.geoplotter.GeoPlotterBase.view_poi` and
:meth:`geovista.geoplotter.GeoPlotterBase.add_graticule`. A quantity is read in
the units of the function's CRS, or in degrees where it takes none. (:user:`claude`)
