Fixed :meth:`geovista.bridge.Transform.from_points` failing for 2D points with
``vectors``, whatever the CRS of the vectors, and for 3D points with or without
them, although it documents both. The points of a cloud are now taken as one
sequence, and a ``zlevel`` array still broadcasts against their shape. Closes
:issue:`2604` and :issue:`2605`. (:user:`claude`)
