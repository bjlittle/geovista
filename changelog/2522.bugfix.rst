Fixed the detection of a mesh that has already been flattened, which previously
relied on :attr:`pyproj.crs.CRS.is_projected`. A flat geographic
:class:`~pyproj.crs.CRS` such as ``+proj=latlong``, which is what
``cartopy >=0.26`` uses for ``ccrs.PlateCarree()``, reports ``is_projected`` as
``False``, so such a mesh was processed as if it were still a sphere.
:func:`geovista.transform.transform_mesh` and
:meth:`geovista.geoplotter.GeoPlotterBase.add_mesh` then sliced the mesh at a
seam and spun its longitude/latitude values with ``rotate_z``, corrupting a
shifted reprojection and desynchronising
:meth:`geovista.geodesic.BBox.enclosed`. ``add_mesh`` with a ``zlevel``
radially rescaled it, and :meth:`geovista.geoplotter.GeoPlotterBase.view_poi`
placed the camera edge-on to the plane. (:user:`claude`)
