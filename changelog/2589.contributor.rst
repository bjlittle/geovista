In a planar CRS, ``transform_mesh`` has lifted a mesh by ``zlevel * zscale``
times a quarter of the mesh's own extent, so equal levels sit at different
heights in a projected scene. The design that fixes it, tying the length to the
Earth's radius in the CRS's own units, is recorded under
``docs/src/developer/specs/`` and cited as ``zlevel spec §…``. (:user:`claude`)
