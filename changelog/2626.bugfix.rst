Fixed :meth:`geovista.bridge.Transform.from_unstructured` transforming a masked
point that no face uses, when the connectivity is given as an array. Such a
point raised ``ProjError`` outside the domain of a bounded projection, such as
``+proj=ortho``, and stretched the bounds of the mesh inside it. It is now left
out of the transform when ``xs`` and ``ys`` carry the same mask, and stays in
the mesh at the position of the first point that is transformed. Closes
:issue:`2625`. (:user:`claude`)
