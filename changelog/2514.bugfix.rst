Fixed :func:`geovista.crs.get_central_meridian` and
:func:`geovista.crs.set_central_meridian`, which recognised the central meridian
only as EPSG parameter ``8802``. A projection method defines its longitudinal
origin using whichever parameter suits its geometry, so eight
:mod:`cartopy.crs` projections silently lost their shift and were seam-cut at
longitude ``0``, tearing the mesh across the projection. Closes :issue:`2512`.
(:user:`claude`)
