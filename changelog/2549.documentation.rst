Vendored the `sphinx-tippy
<https://github.com/sphinx-extensions2/sphinx-tippy>`__ tooltip runtime. The
extension leaves ``tippy_js`` pointing at ``@popperjs/core@2`` and
``tippy.js@6`` on ``unpkg``. Both float on a major version, every reader of
every page fetched them from a third party, and no tooltip appeared at all
when that host was unreachable. Pinned copies of the two ``UMD`` bundles now
sit under ``docs/src/_static/js/``, where sphinx serves them with its own
cache-busting query. Both are ``MIT`` licensed, so each is accompanied by its
upstream notice in a sibling ``<bundle>.LICENSE.txt``, which is published along
with the documentation.

``sphinx-tippy`` also moved from a ``PyPI`` dependency to a ``conda-forge``
one, and the ``conf.py`` import guard that treated it as optional went with
it. That guard let a missing extension strip every tooltip out of the build
and still leave it green. (:user:`claude`)
