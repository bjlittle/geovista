:meth:`geovista.geoplotter.GeoPlotterBase.add_mesh` and
:meth:`geovista.geoplotter.GeoPlotterBase.add_points` take ``height=``,
``depth=`` and ``vertical_units=``, as heights spec §4.2 lists, and place each
point by its height at the scene's globe, on the globe or on a map. The heights
are written to the copy drawn, so the mesh given is left unchanged, and a mesh
that already carries heights takes no more. A mesh with heights built at another
globe is now drawn from its heights at the scene's globe, where it used to keep
its own exaggeration. :attr:`geovista.accessor.GeoVistaAccessor.height` and
:attr:`geovista.accessor.GeoVistaAccessor.depth` now return ``pint`` quantities
in metres. (:user:`claude`)
