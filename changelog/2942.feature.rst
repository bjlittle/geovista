A scene takes heights. :meth:`geovista.geoplotter.GeoPlotterBase.add_mesh` and
:meth:`geovista.geoplotter.GeoPlotterBase.add_points` take ``height=``,
``depth=`` and ``vertical_units=``, as heights spec §4.2 lists, and place each
point by its height at the scene's globe, on the globe and on a map. The mesh
given keeps no heights of them, and one that carries heights already takes
none. A mesh with heights that was built at another globe is now drawn from its
heights at the scene's, where it used to keep its own exaggeration.
:attr:`geovista.accessor.GeoVistaAccessor.height` and
:attr:`geovista.accessor.GeoVistaAccessor.depth` now return ``pint`` quantities
in metres. (:user:`claude`)
