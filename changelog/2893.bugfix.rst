Fixed :func:`geovista.geodesic.line` building a line almost all of NaN points for
a masked or NaN position, or raising a ``TypeError`` that named no argument, and
:class:`geovista.geodesic.BBox` and :func:`geovista.geodesic.wedge` building a
box around a masked or NaN corner. All three now refuse a position whose
longitude or latitude is masked or NaN, naming the argument and counting the
missing positions, and refuse an infinite coordinate. When an infinite coordinate
or height sits in an array of more than one dimension, the refusal now gives its
index in each dimension, such as ``(1, 1)``. This lands row 2 of positions spec §9.
